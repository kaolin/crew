Two things that shape what you're looking at. The token chart comes from transcripts on this Mac, so it misses claude.ai and your other machines; the two percentage charts are account-wide and authoritative on the limit. And the endpoint reports whole-number percentages only — an hour that burned 0.9% of the week reads as 0 — which is why those two look like staircases while the token chart has texture. The quiet floor of 405 tokens an hour is not you: it's the crew-pinger heartbeat, a machine baseline that never stops.
